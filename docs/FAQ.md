# FAQ - platform-navigator

**Q: What problem does this actually solve?**
A: Helps platform teams reduce toil around self-service with a guided workflow and measurable guardrails.

Pain point: Teams still fight slow manual work, unclear ownership, and brittle handoffs when self-service is under pressure.

**Q: Free to run?**
A: Yep. Vercel - $0/mo. See `terraform/INFRA_REQUIREMENTS.md`

**Q: Production ready?**
A: Yes - SLOs, security hardening, DR runbook, Helm, Terraform, monitoring.

**Q: Stack?**
A: Backstage, ArgoCD, Crossplane, Terraform, OPA

**Q: What infra do I need?**
A: `terraform/INFRA_REQUIREMENTS.md` has the full breakdown - CPU/RAM/storage/network/cost.
